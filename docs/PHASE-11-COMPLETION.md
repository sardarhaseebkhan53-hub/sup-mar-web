# Phase 11 — Trust & Safety

QAVLIO now has a transparent trust layer on top of existing seller, chat, and report flows.

- Seller trust profiles and badges only when criteria are met
- Reviews require a real listing conversation; no self or duplicate reviews
- Seller responses, helpful votes, and review reports
- Safety Center at `/safety` with approved policy pages for AI
- Listing risk assessments stay internal; public pages may show a neutral safety notice
- User-level block is enforced on new messages
- Admin review, risk, and expanded report queues
