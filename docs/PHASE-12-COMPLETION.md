# Phase 12 — Advanced Buyer Experience

QAVLIO now has a signed-in buyer layer on top of existing search, listings, chat, and trust flows.

- Favorites with optimistic hearts, guest local saves, login merge, bulk remove, and optional price alerts
- Saved searches with instant, daily, and weekly alerts
- Price history only when a seller actually changes price
- Recently viewed (server for members, local for guests) and private recent searches
- Follow seller, followed-seller alerts, similar listings, and location-aware discovery
- Notification preferences for messages, search alerts, price alerts, seller updates, listing availability, payments, and marketing
- Email-ready `NotificationProvider` keeps credentials on the server
